Author-keyword thesaurus of the 906-record keyword maps

Files
- thesaurus_keywords.json: variant -> canonical keyword.
- plural_merges_DE.json: plural forms merged into the singular because both forms occur among the normalized author
  keywords.

Application to each raw author keyword
1. lower case, collapse white space, replace "optimis" by "optimiz";
2. thesaurus lookup;
3. remove a trailing bracketed acronym, e.g. "unmanned surface vehicle (usv)" -> "unmanned surface vehicle";
4. thesaurus lookup;
5. singular step: a keyword ending in "s" whose form without the final "s" is a thesaurus entry is replaced by that
   entry's value;
6. each keyword is kept once per record; then the plural merges are applied.

Correction (keyword version "corrected")
In step 5 the canonical keyword "ais" (from "automatic identification system", "AIS", 13 records) is shortened to
"ai" and then mapped to "artificial intelligence" through the entry "ai" -> "artificial intelligence". In the
corrected version step 5 is not applied to a keyword that is already a canonical thesaurus value. This changes only
"ais" (checked over all raw author keywords: the other keywords affected by step 5, "colregs" and "autonomous surface
vessels", give the same result in both versions). Result: "artificial intelligence" 16 records and "ais" 13 records
(one record uses both), instead of "artificial intelligence" 28 records.

Raw forms behind every mapped keyword: data/keyword_variants_t5.csv.
